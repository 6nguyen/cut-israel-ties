Researched: 2026-11-04 · Informs: [docs/prd/action-router.md](../prd/action-router.md)

Snapshot taken the evening of 2026-11-04 (US), the day after the midterms. Question: who could replace Chuck Schumer as Senate Democratic leader in the December 2026 caucus vote, or condition their vote on him, and which of them has a stated position on stripping NDAA §1217 (the Senate's US–Israel defense-integration section) and opposing a new MOU. ## State of play (2026-11-04)
- No declared challenger. The Star Tribune (Nov 4): "As of Wednesday, Schumer does not have a challenger in the leadership election." AP, via Newsweek: no senator has stepped forward.
- Schumer says he has the votes: "The issue is not me. I'll be reelected as majority leader. But the issue is focusing on the American people and what they want." (The Hill, Nov 4, 12:49 PM ET.) The Hill projects a caucus of at least 54, possibly 56, in 2027; he needs a majority of it, by secret ballot.
- Vote timing: "next month" (The Hill), "in December before the new Democratic-majority Congress takes office in January" (Star Tribune). No date published. **VERIFY** the date; the PRD placeholder is 2026-12-15.
- Five senators-elect say they will not vote for him: El-Sayed (MI), Jackson (ME), Hamilton (KS), Flanagan (MN), Stratton (IL). Five votes cannot block him; the lever is the other ~50.
- Prediction market (Kalshi, as quoted by Newsweek on Nov 3–4; separately traded contracts, not a poll): Schumer 79%, Schatz 13%, Murphy 6.9%, Van Hollen 4%, Booker 2%.
- Indivisible called on Nov 4 for the caucus to replace Schumer (The Hill, page blocked by a bot wall at fetch time; headline and prior-day coverage only).
- The §1217 record that exists is the July 9, 2026 Dear Colleague letter led by Van Hollen and signed by Sanders, Warren, Markey, Merkley and Welch, urging a no vote on the NDAA motion to proceed until the Israel provisions were debated, plus Van Hollen's amendments 6140 and 6144 to strike §1217 and 6143 to delay it. Cloture failed 50–46 on July 14, 2026 (43 Democrats, King, Sanders and Thune voted no; Fetterman and Padilla did not vote). The amendments were never voted on. No sitting senator has a public statement on a new MOU that this scan could find.

## Ranked top three (paste-ready for `our_picks`)
The goal's unified ask is a condition, not a coronation: senators vote only for a leader who commits to strip §1217 and oppose a new MOU. The picks are the names a caller can put to that condition. Nobody has declared, so every rationale says so.

1. **Chris Van Hollen (MD)** — The only possible leader with a record on the exact vote this goal targets: he led the July 9, 2026 letter against the NDAA's Israel provisions and filed the amendments to strike §1217, and he voted to block both April arms sales. He refuses AIPAC money. Senator-elect El-Sayed named him first ("Chris Van Hollen would be perfect"). He has not sought the job and says his focus is 2028, so the ask is a draft: call your senator and ask them to back Van Hollen, or any leader who makes the same commitment. Attention is mid-tier for a senator (IG 321K; TikTok top clip 3.4M, no own profile found). `source_url`: https://truthout.org/articles/senators-urge-colleagues-to-block-ndaa-until-pro-israel-provisions-are-debated/ · `signal`: "IG 321K followers; TikTok top clip 3.4M (C-SPAN); Kalshi 4%" · `signal_date`: 2026-11-04.
2. **Chris Murphy (CT)** — The most forceful Israel critic among the names markets take seriously (Kalshi 6.9%): "I cannot support this government's actions. They are not aligned with U.S. national security objectives" (Aug 9, 2026), and yes on both arms JRDs. He has no public statement on §1217 or a new MOU, so the condition has to be put to him, not assumed. He is recovering from a stroke; confirm his status before the pick ships. Attention is comparable to Van Hollen's with an active TikTok of his own (IG 311K; TikTok 294.2K followers, top clip 1.2M). `source_url`: https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/murphy-says-israel-not-aligned-204127626.html · `signal`: "IG 311K; TikTok 294.2K followers, top clip 1.2M (C-SPAN)" · `signal_date`: 2026-11-04.
3. **Cory Booker (NJ)** — The only one of the four who has said the quiet part: "it is time, though, for new leadership" and, on running, "Haven't ruled it out" (Nov 2025); he would not commit to Schumer after the midterms. His Israel record is the weakest of the three: his first vote to block an arms sale was April 2026, after voting against the same in 2024 and 2025, and he has nothing on §1217. He carries by far the most attention (IG 3.3M; TikTok 2M followers, top clip 3M), which is why he is here: a Booker pledge to the condition would move the most people. Markets give him 2%. Alternate: Brian Schatz (Kalshi's 13% favourite, yes on arms JRDs, "I'm voting NO on the defense spending bill"), dropped from the three because he is running for whip, not leader, and has the smallest footprint (IG 12.1K, TikTok 42.5K). `source_url`: https://www.foxnews.com/politics/eading-senate-democrat-tells-fox-news-its-time-for-new-leadership-schumer-faces-growing-pressure · `signal`: "IG 3.3M; TikTok 2M followers, top clip 3M (C-SPAN)" · `signal_date`: 2026-11-04.
