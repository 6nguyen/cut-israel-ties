# Outreach — who to ask to amplify the tool, what we offer them, and the first message to each

How the maintainer runs partner and creator outreach from the project
identity. Evidence and tiering are in
`docs/research/social-distribution-kickoff.md` §8; this is the runbook.
Every message goes from the project account or email, never a personal one.

## 0. Before the first message
- A project mailbox (`hello@cutthetiesmovement.org`) and the project
  Instagram account exist and have posted at least three times.
- Analytics and a per-partner link exist, so a partner can be told what
  their share did. Until then, offer the link and promise the number.
- The member page shows a sourced vote line, so the one-sentence pitch is
  true when they click.
- Assets ready to attach: a 7-slide carousel (what the vote is, how to
  look up your member, the 30-second script) and a 15-second screen
  recording of the lookup, both without watermark.

## 1. What we can honestly offer
We have no audience. What we have is a working tool, structured data and
engineering hours. Offers, cheapest for us first:
1. **A named link back.** Their petition, donor lookup or campaign page
   as the `partner_url` on the goal (the "Organise one" line already
   offers it) and in the member page's "more" row. Their traffic, our
   pages.
2. **Attribution.** A link only they post (`/?via=<slug>`), with the
   calls and receipts it drove reported back weekly from the digest.
   Deterministic counts; a creator can quote "my reel drove N calls".
3. **Co-authorship.** Instagram Collab invite on every post built from
   their clip or data, so it lives on their grid with their handle.
4. **Data and build hours.** A CSV of positions and roll calls for their
   graphics; a vote-lookup embed for their site; a goal page carrying
   their campaign's ask verbatim; a per-member call page skinned for
   their campaign. These cost us evenings, which is what we have.
5. **Volunteer work for their campaign.** Position research with sourced
   rows (`draft-position`), whip counts for their target list, clipping
   and captioning, comment-section replies that point back to them.

Never offer money, a paid post, or a share from any personal account.
